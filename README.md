# My Favorite Korean Drama Series

![Dr. Romantic](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt6157190/))

## 1. About the Series

**Dr. Romantic** is a Korean drama about doctors who work at Doldam Hospital. It shows their daily lives, problems, and experiences as they care for their patients. They also perform difficult surgery that no one expects that they can do and the patients can survive.

## 2. Series Information

| Information | Details |
|---|---|
| Title | Dr. Romantic |
| Country | South Korea |
| Genre | Medical Drama, Romance |
| Number of Seasons | 3 |
| Main Character | Kim Sabu |
| Setting | Doldam Hospital |

## 3. Main Characters

- **Kim Sabu** – A skilled doctor who teaches young doctors.
- **Seo Woo-jin** – A doctor who works hard to help his patients.
- **Cha Eun-jae** – A young doctor who wants to improve her skills.
- **Park Eun-tak** – A nurse who really cares for the patients.

## 4. Why I Like This Series

I like *Dr. Romantic* because it has a good story and many touching scenes. I enjoy watching the doctors help their patients and solve problems. It also shows the value of teamwork, kindness, and hard work. They don't just treat patients they really care about them, and unlike in reality that doctors choose who to treat and not to. specially those who don't have the money to pay for their medical bills. because now a days some doctors choose to treat VIP patients than ordinary patients. 

## 5. My Favorite Character

My favorite character is **Dr. Kim**. He is a smart, grumpy, amazing, and really cares for everyone. He's also a great teacher that cares about his students. and He always thinks about his patients.

## 6. Lessons from the Series

> A good doctor does not only treat sickness. A good doctor also cares about people.
> A good doctor does not see money instead find ways to help people in need. 

This series teaches us to:

1. Help people who need us.
2. Work well with others.
3. Keep trying even when things are hard.
4. Always do what is right.
